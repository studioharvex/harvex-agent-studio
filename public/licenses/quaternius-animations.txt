Animation clips in /sim/clips.json and /sim/clips.bin are made from the "Universal Animation Library" (Standard)
by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html), released under
CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They were retargeted to the studio's skeleton and stored as per-bone turns by scripts/build-sim-clips.mjs.
